# OmniDnD — Manual QA

Use these short reviews for human judgment about appearance, comfort and 2014 rules explanations. Automated checks cover arithmetic, persistence and command behavior.

Try your own campaigns and characters, including characters friends might use later. The Fool remains the acceptance fixture in TEST_CHARACTER.md. Leave a review unchecked until a person has assessed it.

## Phase 1 — M3: shell, HUD and local workspace

- [ ] On phone, tablet and desktop, judge whether navigation makes the current campaign and character clear and keeps common destinations easy to find.
- [ ] Scan the HUD during play. HP, temporary HP, concentration and effects should be easy to distinguish, including with long names.
- [ ] Open details with mouse, touch and keyboard. Judge reading space, placement, dismissal and return focus without having to hunt for the next control.
- [ ] Review creation and campaign settings. Rolled scores, standard array, point buy and retained choices should be understandable without explaining the form aloud.
- [ ] Review import, copy and deletion wording on a disposable character. Decide whether the preview and confirmation make the affected character and campaign clear.
- [ ] Read saving, conflict and error messages. Judge whether they explain the next step calmly and distinguish character actions from workspace management.
- [ ] Review Reference and Credits. Edition labels, destinations, attribution and external links should be clear to someone seeking 2014 rules.
- [ ] In a normal browser, judge contrast, fonts, icons, spacing and reduced motion across screen sizes. Note any uncomfortable or distracting interaction.

## Phase 1 — M4: Spellbook

- [ ] Scan spell groups, slots and active concentration. Judge whether availability and spell types are understandable without relying on color alone.
- [ ] Read several summaries for different characters and casting classes. Decide whether saves, attacks, components and source links give enough context for table use.
- [ ] Read a hover preview, pinned detail and phone sheet. Judge whether long descriptions and many slot options remain comfortable to inspect and select.
- [ ] Walk through casting and upcasting. The chosen slot, healing roll, ritual choice and Apply to me option should make the player's choices clear.
- [ ] Review a warning and its exception path. Decide whether Cancel and Do anyway explain the consequence while leaving hits, saves and other creatures' outcomes to the table.
- [ ] Use filters and favorites as you would during a session. Judge discoverability, empty results and whether another character feels free of fixture-specific shortcuts.
- [ ] Review known/prepared spell management and replacement wording. Class limits, retained conflicts and the effect of a choice should be understandable before saving.

## Phase 1 — M5: Combat

- [ ] Start combat and move between acting and waiting. Judge whether the app clearly tracks your character's turn while the DM keeps the initiative order.
- [ ] Use action tabs and movement controls during a short encounter. Judge whether actions, bonus actions, reactions and resources can be scanned quickly on a phone.
- [ ] Read the bonus-action-spell warning and Show all explanation. Confirm the wording communicates the 2014 restriction and leaves exceptions explicit.
- [ ] Review reactions and Readied responses. The app should help record a declared choice without implying that a trigger, hit or target outcome was decided automatically.
- [ ] Review damage, concentration and death-save prompts. Natural rolls, external modifiers and explicit Pass/Fail choices should feel clear under pressure.
- [ ] Compare concentration with effects on you. Decide whether self versus ally targets, remaining game time and source relationships are understandable.
- [ ] Read combat history and end an encounter. Judge whether entries explain what happened and whether history, Undo and the next useful control remain easy to reach.

## Phase 1 — M6: Rest and Dashboard

- [ ] Scan Dashboard on several characters. Resource groups, empty states and separate class or item instances should be understandable without remembering fixture details.
- [ ] Choose quick-access favorites. Judge whether owned, unavailable and reference-only entries explain what can be used and why.
- [ ] Read conditions and effect sources. Decide whether the presentation makes clear which source is responsible and what ending it means.
- [ ] Review Short Rest while deciding whether to spend another Hit Die. The healing preview and your bard's or another bard's Song of Rest choices should support that decision.
- [ ] Review Long Rest recovery and interval warnings. Hit Dice recovery and the prepared-spell review should be understandable; food, sleep and interruptions remain table judgments.
- [ ] Advance game time with presets and a custom duration. Judge whether elapsed time, day changes and expired effects are clear without suggesting real-world time passes automatically.
- [ ] On phone, tablet and desktop, read complete rest previews and nested warnings. Judge scrolling, action placement and focus when moving into spell preparation.

## Phase 1 — M7: Character Sheet and Exploration

- [ ] Read the Sheet for your own character. Judge whether identity, class choices, proficiencies, tools, senses and conditional defenses are easy to find and described honestly.
- [ ] Inspect calculated numbers by mouse, touch and keyboard. Judge whether source parts, locked HP entries and long high-level explanations remain readable and reachable.
- [ ] Compare skills and passives on Sheet and Explore. Proficiency, Expertise and Jack of All Trades explanations should distinguish checks from saving throws.
- [ ] Compare ordinary Inspiration with Bardic Inspiration. Decide whether the labels and resulting history make their different purposes obvious.
- [ ] Use Explore on phone and desktop. Judge utility/ritual choices, clock previews and effect timers; distinguish ritual labels from your class's casting eligibility.
- [ ] Review level-up in XP and milestone campaigns. Judge whether the roll/average choice, HP and Hit Dice preview, method warning and recorded-level wording match the campaign without asking for derived numbers.
- [ ] On phone, tablet and desktop, judge whether the compact status/log row leaves enough room for play. Find one Undo across character screens, spell management and full history; judge whether its latest-change wording makes the result clear.
- [ ] Edit your own character and a prepared caster. Judge Save/Cancel wording, retained grants and source choices, especially when changing race, background, subclass or Expertise.
- [ ] Apply a noted override and inspect ✎ and its breakdown across screens. Judge whether calculated versus overridden values, Reset and normal spending after a remaining correction are understandable, including large resource totals.
- [ ] Review rolled, standard-array and point-buy entry on your own character. Base scores, separate bonus sources, point cost and ✎ previews should make the result clear before saving.

## Phase 1 — M8: hardening

- [ ] Install from your phone and desktop browser, then reopen the installed app offline. Judge its icon, fonts, navigation and save feedback on the actual device.
- [ ] Judge whether activating updates only after all app windows close fits your session habits, especially while editing a character.
- [ ] Run a short keyboard session. Judge whether focus stays obvious while correcting HP, choosing a spell, closing nested details and using Undo.
- [ ] Compare available, used, depleted and disabled controls in Combat. Judge whether text and state markers remain clear without relying on color alone.
- [ ] Try reduced motion on your actual devices. Judge comfort while opening previews and sheets and changing resources.
- [ ] When Settings shows Saving, judge whether the temporary pause and return focus make the next usable control clear.
- [ ] Review Reset on your own character. Judge whether its preview, confirmation, retained belongings/time and backup/Undo wording make the scope clear before applying.
- [ ] Play a full session with The Fool and try a friend's character. Judge combat pace, clear rule warnings and manual DM outcomes across screens.

## Phase 2 — M9: Inventory and Features

- [ ] Browse a character with duplicate items and nested containers on phone and desktop. Judge whether notes, locations and empty quantities distinguish each copy clearly.
- [ ] Record your actual outfit and review armor replacements, proficiency warnings and time/action reminders. Judge whether the changed AC, attacks and shared Undo make the result clear.
- [ ] Add a catalogue item, edit its notes/location and review removal. Judge the bundle count, source warnings, Cancel and retained-contents wording before confirming.
- [ ] Adjust coins and review carried weight. Judge denomination previews, unknown-weight wording and capacity warnings against your character's actual load and table rules.
- [ ] Review recorded attunement bonds, duplicate copies and depleted items. Judge the limit, DM warnings and focused-rest reminders before marking or ending a bond.
- [ ] Drink a potion in combat, have an ally give one at 0 HP, and throw acid. Judge the action cost, roll entry and the table-resolved wording.
- [ ] Create a homebrew weapon and a charged trinket, then edit and export them. Judge the form, the based-on choice and whether the attack matches.
- [ ] Open Features for your own character. Judge the grouping, long-text folding and whether Use, pips and favorites feel right next to Combat and Home.
- [ ] Open a friend's non-Bard character (Fighter, Monk, Cleric). Judge whether Rage, Ki, Channel Divinity and similar uses match what they track on paper.
- [ ] Spend wand charges, pass a dawn and enter the regain roll. Judge the prompt wording, Skip/Set choices and whether charge rules match your table.

## Phase 2 — MA: Accounts (after hosting, see HOSTING.md)

- [ ] Create an account, confirm the email and sign in on a phone and a laptop. Judge whether the Account section explains what stays on the device.
- [ ] Change a character on one device, press Sync now on both, and check the change, including Undo history, arrives intact.

## Phase 2 — M10: Effects editor and Conditions

- [ ] Add an effect from a real spell or potion during play on your phone. Judge whether the editor is quick enough at the table.
- [ ] Set an effect to end at the start of your next turn, then run a turn. Judge whether it ends when your table expects.
- [ ] Turn on each condition one by one. Check the roll reminders and condition notes against your 2014 Player's Handbook.
- [ ] Turn on prone and invisible together. Judge whether the situational lines and the cancel-out note read clearly.
- [ ] Set exhaustion 1 to 6. Check the level list and the speed and HP limits match how your DM runs exhaustion.

## Phase 2 — M11: Campaign Setup sources and optional rules

- [ ] Open Campaign Setup for your campaign and toggle books and optional rules. Judge whether the list of kept choices from a removed book is clear before saving.
- [ ] Switch to gritty realism or epic heroism and open Short and Long Rest previews. Judge whether the changed rest length makes sense without checking a reference.
- [ ] Enable variant encumbrance and load items near the thresholds. Judge whether the Inventory weight status, Sheet speed and Dashboard reminders make the penalty obvious.
- [ ] Enable potions as a bonus action and drink one in combat. Judge whether the economy cost and the "give" distinction match your table's expectation.
- [ ] Turn encumbrance Off and confirm the Inventory still shows weight facts without capacity warnings or speed penalties.

## Phase 2 — M12: Companions

- [ ] Add your real pet or mount. Judge whether the card shows what you need at a glance on a phone.
- [ ] In combat, press Command: Bonus and check the economy bar spends the bonus action as your table expects.
- [ ] Damage a companion to 0 HP. Judge whether "destroyed" and the Resummon option fit how your DM rules it.
- [ ] Long rest, then check companion uses recharge when you expect.

## Deferred rules review — M5

Q21's instant-death threshold still uses damage remaining after temporary HP. Assisted shows a DM-review note when interpretations differ; Manual suppresses that reminder.

- [ ] At project end, review D80 (Q22) and Q21 with the DM, including damage at zero HP and instant-death thresholds. Record rulings before changing behavior.
